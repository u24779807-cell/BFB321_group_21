SMART SPAZA

User Guide

Smart Spaza helps small food businesses monitor their inventory, identify products approaching expiry and determine when stock may need to be reordered.

Add a Product

Select Add Product and enter the product's stock, expiry and supply-chain information.

Search Inventory

Use the search field to quickly find a specific product.

Monitor Expiry

Products within seven days of their expiry date are marked as Expiring Soon.

Supply Chain Calculations

The system calculates EOQ, safety stock and reorder point using the inventory model.

Flag Products

Products can be flagged if they are spoiled, damaged or have another condition requiring attention.
